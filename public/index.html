About Sidhh Listener

Sidhh Listener was created around one simple idea: everyone deserves someone who will genuinely listen. We are a listening and emotional support service — a place to think out loud, process your day, or simply feel less alone. Our listeners are trained to listen with patience and without judgment.

Important: Sidhh Listener is a listening/support service. It is not psychological counseling, therapy, or medical treatment, and our listeners are not licensed medical or mental health professionals. If you are in crisis, experiencing a medical emergency, or thinking of harming yourself, please contact local emergency services or a qualified mental health professional immediately.
