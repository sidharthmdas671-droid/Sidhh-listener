Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Sidhh Listener ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and booking service, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

When you book a session, we collect your name, age, gender, place, phone number, email address (if provided), your chosen session and time slot, and any optional message you share with us. Payment is processed securely by Razorpay; we do not store your card, UPI, or bank details.

2. How we use your information

We use this information to confirm and manage your booking, contact you about your session, send booking confirmations by email, and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Confidentiality of conversations

Anything you share with your listener during a session is treated as confidential, except where we are legally required to act — for example, where there is a genuine risk of serious harm to you or someone else.

4. Data sharing

We share limited booking information with our payment processor (Razorpay) solely to process payments, and with email service providers solely to send booking confirmations.

5. Data retention

We retain booking records for as long as reasonably necessary for record-keeping, support, and legal/accounting purposes.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us through the WhatsApp button on our website.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out to us via the "Chat on WhatsApp" button on our homepage.
