Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Cancelling a session

If you need to cancel or reschedule your session, please contact us via the "Chat on WhatsApp" button at least 4 hours before your scheduled time.

2. Refunds

Full refunds are provided if you cancel at least 4 hours before your session. Cancellations made less than 4 hours before the session, or missed sessions without notice, are not eligible for a refund.

3. Rescheduling

You may reschedule your session to another available slot at no extra cost, subject to availability, if requested at least 4 hours in advance.

4. Technical or payment issues

If a payment is deducted but your booking does not show as confirmed, please contact us with your payment reference and we will verify and resolve it promptly, including a refund if the session cannot be honored.

5. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method via Razorpay and typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

6. Contact

For any cancellation or refund request, please reach out via the "Chat on WhatsApp" button on our homepage.
